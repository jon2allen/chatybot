Here are six famous foods from Germany and the cities they are most associated with. Each has its own unique history and preparation method.

Bratwurst in Nuremberg is famous for its small, thin sausages. They are often served in sets of six or twelve with sauerkraut and mustard. 

Currywurst in Berlin is an iconic street food. It consists of steamed and fried pork sausage. The sausage is sliced and topped with a spiced ketchup-curry sauce, often served with fries. Berlin is the best place to try it.

Brezel, also known as pretzels, are a Bavarian staple. The giant, soft, salted pretzels are particularly famous in Munich. They are often enjoyed with beer.

Sauerbraten from the Rhineland region is a pot roast. It is marinated for days in vinegar and spices. This dish is traditionally served with red cabbage and dumplings. The Rhineland region is known for this dish.

Black Forest Cake, or Schwarzwälder Kirschtorte, originated in Baden-Württemberg. This decadent chocolate cake features cherries, whipped cream, and kirsch. Kirsch is a cherry brandy that gives it a distinctive flavor.

Labskaus is a traditional seafarer's meal from Hamburg and Northern Germany. It is a hearty dish made from corned beef, potatoes, beets, and onions. Labskaus is often served with a fried egg and pickles.
