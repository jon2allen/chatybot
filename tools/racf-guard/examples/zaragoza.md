# Authors from Zaragoza, Spain

## Javier Sierra (born 1971)
- **Notable Works:** *The Secret Supper* (2004), *The Covenant of the Magus* (2006), and other historical‑mystery novels.
- **Why He's Notable:** Known for blending esoteric history, religion, and adventure in fast‑paced, internationally bestselling novels. His works often explore hidden truths and secret societies, earning him a prominent place in contemporary Spanish literature.

## Ignacio Martínez de Pisón (born 1960)
- **Notable Works:** *The Infamous Angel* (2000), *The Sound of the Sea* (2006), *The End of the World* (2011), and numerous short stories and essays.
- **Why He's Notable:** A versatile writer who has received critical acclaim, including Spain’s Premio de la Crítica. His novels delve into memory, family, and the passage of time, showcasing a distinctive literary voice that resonates both domestically and abroad.

Both authors hail from (or grew up in) Zaragoza and have made significant contributions to Spanish literature, each with a unique style and a broad readership worldwide.
